For nonempty text, denominator is stripped Python code-point length, **including** internal spaces, punctuation, quotes and newlines. `/1k` divides by `max(len(text)/1000, .001)`.
- Dialogue numerator is the sum of captured interiors, excluding quote delimiters; regex accepts any opening `“ " 『 「` with any closing `” " 』 」`, interior length **1–200**, including newlines. - Paragraph splitting treats each nonblank newline-delimited segment as a paragraph. Average is **whole stripped text length / nonblank segment count**, not mean stripped segment length; opener variety is unique first-two-code-point strings / paragraph count. - Endings use independent substring `text.count` sums for `했다, 이었다, 였다` and four tilde-prefixed weak phrases. No sentence-final boundary/morphology.
